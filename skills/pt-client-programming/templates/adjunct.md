**Purpose:** [[CONFIRM: stay connected to the team while rebuilding consistent, symptom-stable tolerance and preparing for later-season performance.]]

> **Success is not finishing every item.** Success means choosing the right dose, keeping symptoms from spreading, and being ready for the next training day.

# 1. How to use this plan

- Choose your Green, Yellow, or Red status in the main program before every session.
- Choose the current stage in this adjunct. Stay at that stage until two sessions and both next mornings are Green.
- All sport, PE, hiking, and lower-body strength count toward total load.
- Use no more than one organised sport in a day, no more than two impact days in a row, and at least one full rest day each week.
- Do not make up missed distance or conditioning. Resume from the last stage handled well.

## Share with coaches

- The athlete owns the range, pace, and traffic-light decision.
- The next-morning response matters more than finishing the written workout.
- No hard intensity, testing, or extra conditioning until the matching stage is Green.
- Competition return follows successful practice exposure and a shared decision with the athlete, family, coach, {{clinician_first_name}}, and the medical team.

> **Stop and contact the care team** for symptoms that spread, new numbness or tingling, weakness, giving-way, a gait change, or sharp or rapidly increasing pain.
