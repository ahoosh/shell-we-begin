# Adjusting challenge and recovery

Your body adapts to the right challenge, and recovery is part of the process. A yellow day means changing the dose, not failure. Use less range, resistance, time, or support, then build again when movement, confidence, and the later/next-day response return to green.

Change one variable at a time: comfortable range, control, repetitions, resistance, complexity, then speed or activity-specific demand.

Total load includes rehabilitation, [[CONFIRM: this client's work, chores, sport, travel]], and sleep. A busy day may need a shorter routine.

Do not double the program after a missed day. Resume the usual dose that matches your current response.
